Files are from the mirror's BodyParts3D 3.0 dataset, converted from OBJ to STL by the mirror author. Source FMA identifiers:

- humerus.stl — FMA23130, right humerus
- radius.stl — FMA23464, right radius
- ulna.stl — FMA23467, right ulna
- biceps-short.stl — FMA37684, short head of right biceps brachii
- biceps-long.stl — FMA37686, long head of right biceps brachii
- brachialis.stl — FMA37668, right brachialis
- brachioradialis.stl — FMA38486, right brachioradialis

Application adaptations: coordinate-frame registration, unit conversion, vertex welding, recomputed normals, length scaling, and muscle deformation. These adapted mesh assets are distributed under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. Source STLs are retained here. The registration landmarks are approximate, not clinically validated.
